# Genus Rite V5: Old Edge With Directed Grounding

This is the canonical content of the artefact genus promoted from the
`exp072-report-quality/post-gate-grounding-arm/run-v1` experiment. It is a
thinking rite, not an HTML template and not a report-only shape.

## Baseline Gate

Every substantial artefact must make these readable in its own vehicle:

1. Identity of the thing being decided or tested.
2. Factuality: observed result, inference, and recommendation stay separate.
3. Reader context: enough reconstruction for Lucas/Ed without taxing what he already holds.
4. Mechanism: at least one concrete case, path, trace, row, diff, or representative instance.
5. Mundo: external terms or practices with fit/mismatch, changing a claim or next decision.
6. Lineage: prior artefacts numbered, located, and evaluated for inheritance/rejection/change.
7. Utility: decision, caveats, and next validation.

## Promoted Delta

The winning arm added the old Edge stance plus a post-gate grounding phase:

1. The first draft follows an old-edge equivalent rite: derivation, honest lacunas, outside-frame
   instinct, what-I-do-not-know, lineage, mechanism, and mentor arc.
2. The gate is not just approval. It produces actionable lacunas tied to specific claims.
3. Mundo grounding happens after the gate and answers those lacunas. It must not decorate the text.
4. The final rewrite shows the grounding effect: what changed in claim, caveat, example, decision,
   or next validation.
5. The final close fact-audits external transfer. Sources can position a local result in a method
   family without validating its effect size, saturation band, rank, or causality.

## Review Signal From The Experiment

The corrected report was accepted because it preserved:

- explicit lineage table;
- concrete mechanism trace;
- control-negative or otherwise falsifying example when available;
- visible post-gate repositioning;
- narrow architectural claim;
- next experiment / validation step;
- honest caveat that external comparators do not remove local small-N or judge-dependence risk.

The useful default is not a fixed layout. The invariant is the movement:

`old-edge draft -> actionable gap gate -> directed grounder -> rewrite with visible delta -> fact-audited close`

For a map, the delta may be edges or captions. For a plan, it may be changed dependencies or validation
steps. For a prototype, it may be interaction notes or before/after behavior. For a report, it may be
prose, tables, callouts, or a "Grounding effect" block. The vehicle is free; the movement is not.
