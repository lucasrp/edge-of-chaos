# The mentee-language glossary (Idiom) is removed; CONTEXT.md is the edge's internal self-model, organized by module

## Context

The grill maintained an **Idiom** — a standing-page glossary of the *mentee's* own language, kept
current by the grill's "afinamento" face. It did not catch on and did not earn its keep. It is distinct
from the edge's **own** glossary (`CONTEXT.md`), which the operator judged importantíssimo and keeps —
the two were being conflated when "drop the glossary" was first proposed.

## Decision

- The **Idiom** (mentee-language glossary) is **removed**. The grill stops keeping a lexicon of the
  mentee's words — it is a strategic mentor, not the mentee's lexicographer.
- **Standing pages** drop from three to two: **Direction** and **Source roadmap** (Idiom out); so
  `wiki_render` projects two, not three.
- **Voz** shrinks to correction + directive — it no longer subsumes Idiom/language.
- The edge's **internal glossary (`CONTEXT.md`) stays** and is refactored into the module-organized
  **self-model**: the 9 modules, each with a responsibility + interface + the concepts inside it — the
  edge about *itself*, never about the mentee.

## Consequences

- The grill's identity sharpens: strategic thinking, not vocabulary-keeping.
- Removing Idiom is a concrete deletion the Curadoria (Module 6) refactor carries; the machine-language
  verbosity and the `*Avoid:*` lists left CONTEXT.md in the same pass.
- `CONTEXT.md` is now injected as the module map, not a 533-line lexicon.
